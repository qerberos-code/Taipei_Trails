# Taipei Trail Finder

Pick a run or hike in Taipei that fits your distance (3 / 5 / 10 km), your time on the trail (30 min to half day), and how far you are willing to travel from your hotel.

- Single file: open `index.html` in any browser, or serve it with GitHub Pages.
- Set your start point by picking the nearest MRT station, or paste a Google Maps link / coordinates for your hotel.
- 18 curated routes: city loops, riverside runs, and mountain hikes from Elephant Mountain to Qixing Mountain.
- Time estimates: run = pace x km + 6 min per 100 m climb; hike = 15 min/km + 10 min per 100 m climb (Naismith's rule).
- Navigate opens Google Maps transit directions from your hotel to the trailhead.

Distances and climbs are approximate. Check conditions and weather before you go.
